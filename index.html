About Inera Doctor Hub

A rule-based clinical decision support system built by INERA GROUP (Inera Software Pvt. Ltd.) for teaching and clinical assistance. It runs entirely in the browser: no server, no API keys, no data leaves the device. Knowledge base: .

Scope and limits: adult first-line management only. Paediatric doses are not auto-calculated. Imaging is not interpreted. Every prescription must be reviewed, edited if needed, and signed by a registered medical practitioner.
